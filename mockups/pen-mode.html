1 · Pen mode (Paper)

Lined sheet: a 1 px rule every 40 px in the app's own rule color, and no margin line. Handwriting stays exactly where it was written. Typed entries sit on the same lines. The bullet you draw gets tinted in its type's color after recognition.

2 · Same page in Type mode

Everything above, as text. The sketch shows as a small drawing in place. A tiny gray dot marks lines that came from handwriting; it's optional and could be removed.

3 · Pen mode (Graphite)

Switch: the capsule beside the title, keyboard | pen. Only on iPad; the Mac always opens in Type mode.
Toolbar: write, draw (kept as a drawing, never converted), eraser, lasso | ink, red, blue, green | undo.
Rows: 40 px, a little taller than typed lines so handwriting has room. Writing further right on a row indents the entry.
